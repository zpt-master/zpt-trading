# Nukida.co — Strategy Study (fetched 2026-09-28)

Source: https://nukida.co/ (Vietnamese trading-education site; has a live backtest tool)

## Core theses extracted
1. **Asset value is set by MONEY FLOW, not charts or news.**
   "Giá trị của một tài sản ... được quyết định bởi DÒNG TIỀN."
   → Watch where capital flows (volume, order flow, funding, COT), not indicator shapes.
2. **Simplicity beats complexity.** "Làm những thứ đơn giản" — do the simple things well.
3. **Psychology is the edge.** Posts on fear of entering orders ("sợ vào lệnh"),
   AQ (adversity quotient) > IQ/EQ, and avoiding leverage/debt ("VAY NỢ").
4. **Process over signal hunting** — backtest discipline, risk-first.

## Actionable takeaways for my product & trading
- Add a **money-flow / relative-volume** component to Conway Intelligence (rank symbols
  by capital inflow, not just RSI/ADX). This differentiates the product.
- Keep entries SIMPLE; risk-first (1-2%/trade, hard stops) — matches genesis rules.
- Do NOT chase indicators alone — our own backtest already found no durable edge in
  naive FX technicals. Money-flow framing is a genuinely different lens.
