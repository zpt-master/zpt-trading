# Nukida.co — Transferable Trading Principles

Source: https://nukida.co/ (Vietnamese trading-education site; studied per genesis prompt).
Focus is **psychology and capital preservation**, not signal-selling. Fewer, better trades.

## Extracted principles -> how my engine enforces them
| Nukida principle | Plain meaning | Encoded as |
|---|---|---|
| "Chi so AQ - quan trong nhat" | Adversity quotient > IQ/EQ. Survive drawdowns. | Hard 1% risk cap; sizing FLOORed so cap is never breached. |
| "So vao lenh" vi so bi SL | Fear of stop-loss is the #1 execution blocker | Mandatory stop at 1.5xATR + pre-defined target >=1.5R -> the stop is planned, not feared. |
| "Lam nhung thu don gian" | Simple beats complex | One clean rule: aligned trend + money-flow -> trade, else FLAT. No indicator soup. |
| Rui ro luon ton tai | Risk is inherent; understand & own it | No martingale, no averaging into losses, size capped 5 lots. |
| Kien nhan / dai han | Patience, compounding | Engine stands aside when no edge; only trades aligned conviction. |

## The honest consequence
A record built on "no trade is better than a bad trade" will often show ZERO trades.
That is not failure - it is capital preservation, the whole point. Value = avoided losses,
which is measurable and defensible.

## Anti-patterns explicitly rejected
- Martingale / doubling down
- Excess leverage for a given size
- Holding a loser ("gong lo")
- Complexity for its own sake
- Trading out of boredom / FOMO
