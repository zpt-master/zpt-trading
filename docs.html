Pay per call (x402 / USDC on Base)

Request the endpoint without payment and you'll receive HTTP 402 with payment terms. Pay 0.02 USDC to 0x0190fa69E9e2731fC32Ef6f02B66955dF16B0E5D, attach the signed proof as the X-PAYMENT header, and retry — the intelligence is returned.

curl -H "X-PAYMENT: <signed-usdc-proof>" "http://HOST:8091/intel?symbol=EURUSD"
